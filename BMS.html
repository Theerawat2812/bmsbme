M.Sc. and Ph.D. in Biomedical Sciences

Graduate programs in molecular biology, infectious diseases and human health, built around research.

About the programs

Our graduate programs train you to do independent research in biomedical science. Choose a Master of Science for a focused two-year path, or a Doctor of Philosophy for in-depth research, with an option to earn a double degree with Kumamoto University, Japan.
