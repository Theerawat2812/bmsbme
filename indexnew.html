How you will learn

Teaching is led by active researchers. Your studies move from strong scientific foundations towards independent research and real-world problems.

  • FoundationsCore principles in the life sciences, quantitative methods and engineering fundamentals.
  • SpecialisationAdvanced modules, laboratory and design practice, and elective study in your areas of interest.
  • ResearchAn independent project supervised by academic staff, presented and assessed at an academic standard.
  • Beyond the degreePreparation for postgraduate study, research careers, healthcare technology and industry.
