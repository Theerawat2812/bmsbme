About the department

Two programs, one shared community. We teach the science of the human body, build the technology that supports it, and bring both together alongside the Faculty of Medicine.

Learning here is led by active researchers and grounded in laboratory, design and clinical practice, from your first year through to an independent research project.

Curriculum

How you will learn

Teaching is led by active researchers. Your studies move from strong scientific foundations towards independent research and real-world problems.

  1. Foundations

    Core principles in the life sciences, quantitative methods and engineering fundamentals.

  2. Specialisation

    Advanced modules, laboratory and design practice, and elective study in your areas of interest.

  3. Research

    An independent project supervised by academic staff, presented and assessed at an academic standard.

  4. Beyond the degree

    Preparation for postgraduate study, research careers, healthcare technology and industry.
