Application requirements

Prepare the following documents before you apply online.

Documents required

  • A scanned copy of the degree certificate(s) (with officially certified English translation)
  • A scanned copy of the academic transcript(s) (with officially certified English translation)
  • A scanned copy of the main passport page showing personal details (if any)
  • A scanned copy of an English proficiency certificate (TOEFL or IELTS)
  • Ph.D. applicants must meet an English language proficiency requirement. Please check at admission.psu.ac.th
  • Other documents which may be required by each program

Please note

  • All supporting documents must be in English, and must have been officially issued and certified by the respective university, government office, or embassy.
  • Documents uploaded via the online admission system should be scanned and combined into a PDF, ZIP or RAR file (formats allowed in ZIP / RAR: JPG, GIF, TIF, PNG, PDF).
  • Two files can be uploaded. Each file must not exceed 5 MB.
